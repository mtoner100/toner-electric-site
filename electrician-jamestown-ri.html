Working in Jamestown

What we see most in Jamestown

Jamestown sits on Conanicut Island, and the electrical work reflects it. Salt air is hard on outdoor equipment, meter sockets and service masts corrode faster than they do inland, and a good number of properties are seasonal homes that sit empty for months at a time.

  • RI-licensed electricians, fully insured
  • Free estimates on new work
  • Speak to an owner when you call during business hours
  • We leave your home or business neat and clean
Common jobs

Electrical work in Jamestown

Corrosion on exterior equipment

Meter sockets, service masts, outdoor receptacles and disconnects take a beating from salt air and need replacing sooner than inland.

Seasonal and second homes

Systems that sit idle for months, then get switched on all at once. Worth checking before the season rather than during it.

Service upgrades for older cottages

Many shoreline properties still run on services sized for a very different era of electrical demand.

Outdoor and dock lighting

Weather-rated fixtures, GFCI protection and wiring specified for a coastal environment.

We cross the bridge for Jamestown work regularly, and we bring what the job needs so it does not turn into a second trip.
